Medicine price data on MedCost India (public/data/medicines/, data/generated/medicines.json)

Licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/), because it
is derived from:
  - "A-Z Medicine Dataset of India" (Kaggle, shudhanshusingh), CC BY-SA 4.0,
    November 2022 snapshot -- brand names, manufacturers, pack sizes and listed MRPs.
Also includes:
  - Jan Aushadhi product list and Kendra list, Pradhan Mantri Bhartiya
    Janaushadhi Pariyojana (PMBI), janaushadhi.gov.in
  - Ceiling prices notified by the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority,
    nppaindia.nic.in
Salt names were checked against NRCeS Common Drug Codes for India (C-DAC Pune,
CC BY 4.0); that data is not republished here.

Prices are for information only and may be out of date. Not medical advice.
